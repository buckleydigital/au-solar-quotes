Rebates & incentives

How the incentives usually work

Programs and amounts change, and eligibility depends on your property. This is the shape of it - your matched installer confirms exactly what applies to you, in writing, as part of the quote.

Applied at the quote

The federal small-scale incentive is almost always taken off the price upfront by the installer, rather than claimed back by you later.

It varies by location

The value is tied to how much energy a system is expected to generate where you live, so the same system is worth a different amount in different parts of the country.

It steps down over time

The federal scheme reduces on a set schedule, so the incentive attached to an install is generally worth less each year it is put off.

Accreditation is the gate

Incentives only apply when the install is done by an accredited installer to the required standard. Every installer we match you with meets that bar.

What's different about going solar in Rockhampton

A couple of things worth knowing before you compare quotes for a Rockhampton property.

Your network is Ergon Energy. The regional Queensland network, and like other regional distributors it can sit below the standard cap on weaker rural lines - worth confirming for your street specifically.
The incentive. The federal incentive is set by the Clean Energy Regulator's postcode zones, which weight further-north postcodes more generously than the southern capitals - your installer confirms the exact figure for this postcode as part of the quote.
Local conditions. The further north you go, the more cyclone-rated mounting matters - ask whether the system your installer specs meets the wind rating for this area.
Sizing. The export limit above caps what Ergon Energy lets you send back to the grid, not what you can install - most homes fit a larger system than they can export and simply use the surplus in the house.
